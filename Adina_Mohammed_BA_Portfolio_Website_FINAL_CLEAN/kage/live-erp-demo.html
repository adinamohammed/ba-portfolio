A small trainer dashboard is useful because trainers need visibility of their assigned PT clients, today’s sessions, pending/completed sessions, progress notes, and own commission status. ⚑ BA Insight — Finance Business Rules

Amount cannot be blank, zero, or negative. PaymentPurpose classifies revenue into membership, PT, freelancer, and maintenance streams. InvoiceNumber must be unique per transaction. Direct membership payments can be for 1, 3, 6, or 12 months and the fee can be negotiated by Owner/Admin. PT package payments can be for 12, 16, 20, or 24 sessions and can also be negotiated. Only Paid and approved revenue-related payments should feed reporting aggregates. Pending, Overdue, and Refunded items should be handled separately.

⚑ BA Insight — Freelancer Billing Rules

Freelancer billing is monthly and access-fee based — not session-based. Private coaching income collected directly by the freelancer is outside KAGE billing scope. If payment is overdue beyond the threshold, Gate Flag becomes active, restricting facility access. Admin can override with a recorded payment arrangement. This separation keeps KAGE revenue reporting clean.

⚑ BA Insight — Renewal Business Rules

Renewal Due flag triggers 7 days before expiry. Expired members remain visible for follow-up tracking — they should not be deleted. Follow-up Owner field provides accountability. WhatsApp/SMS automation is outside MVP; manual calls and logged notes are the accepted process. Renewed members are preserved in the queue for historical view.

⚑ BA Insight — Equipment Business Rules

Every maintenance log links to one equipment item. RepairCost must be zero or greater. Repeated repairs can trigger a ReplacementReviewFlag — but the system only flags; owner/admin makes the final replacement decision. This separation of system suggestion and human decision is an important business rule.

⚑ BA Insight — Reporting Business Rules

RevenueReport is a reporting view, not a transaction table. Owner, Admin, and Finance roles can see full revenue. Trainer users should only see their assigned sessions and commission status — never full revenue. Empty export filters must be blocked to prevent unscoped data exports. Reporting depends on clean transaction data upstream.

Interview Talking Points
I created this prototype to demonstrate BA thinking visually — not just through written documents. A stakeholder can interact with the screens and immediately understand the intended system behaviour.
I selected modules based on operational pain points in gym management: member churn, PT scheduling conflicts, freelancer billing complexity, and untracked equipment costs.
I kept freelancer billing access-fee based to clearly separate KAGE facility revenue from freelancer private coaching income — this is a key business rule that affects revenue reporting accuracy.
Trainer allocation rules include availability, load, client goal, and continuity because PT scheduling involves human judgment, not just slot availability.
I kept WhatsApp/SMS automation outside MVP to avoid over-scoping. The prototype flags this boundary clearly so stakeholders understand what is and is not in scope.
In a real project, I would validate every screen with stakeholders through structured walkthroughs, feedback sessions, and UAT cycles before development begins.
