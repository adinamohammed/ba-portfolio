How I Work

BA Approach — 5-Step Method

The structured approach applied consistently across all three portfolio projects.

1
Understand the Problem
Define the business context, user pain points, and what success looks like before any documentation begins.
2
Map Current Workflow
Document the as-is process, identify friction points, and understand where the gaps are for each stakeholder group.
3
Define MVP Scope
Establish a realistic scope boundary — what is in, what is out, and what is deferred to future phases.
4
Document Requirements & Data
Produce structured artefacts: requirements register, data dictionary, business rules, and process documentation.
5
Validate via UAT & Prototype
Write UAT scenarios, define acceptance criteria, and communicate the solution through annotated prototypes.
